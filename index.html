What is ADAPt?

ADAPt is FAO Papua New Guinea’s public early-warning dashboard for El Niño-related agricultural drought risk. It is built around the Combined Drought Index (CDI), developed by FAO together with PNG’s National Weather Service (NWS), which combines six climate, rainfall, soil-moisture and vegetation signals into one score from 0 to 1 every month.

Use this tab to understand how the CDI is calculated. The Overview tab gives the national picture, and the Province, District and LLG tabs each have their own interactive map and table.

FAO and PNG National Weather Service

About the Combined Drought Index (CDI)

The Combined Drought Index (CDI) assesses drought severity and triggers anticipatory action, complementing the National Weather Service’s existing Drought Early Warning System. It is based on published research (Isaev E, Yuave N, Inape K, Jones C, Dawa L & Sidle RC, 2024, Agricultural Drought-Triggering for Anticipatory Action in Papua New Guinea, Water 16, 2009) and is calculated every month from free, open data sources.

CDI by district

Each district shows the average of the ~5 km CDI pixels inside its boundary. Half of the CDI (El Niño, Indian Ocean and the rainfall forecast) is the same across a whole province, so differences between districts come from rainfall, soil moisture and vegetation. Use this to decide where to look first, not as an exact local value. Boundaries: PNG NSO 2011 census (87 districts). Population: NSO 2024 census.

CDI-based response guidance

The Combined Drought Index (CDI) phase is ADAPt's primary, government-endorsed trigger for anticipatory action — see the Province, District and LLG tabs for each area's current phase.

CDI phaseRecommended action
Readiness
CDI 0.40–0.59		Maintain preparedness while supporting higher-phase operations elsewhere.
Anticipatory Action
CDI 0.60–0.79		Activate/scale anticipatory action now; complete field verification and prioritize hotspots.
Response threshold
CDI 0.80–1.00		Urgently verify severity and impacts; transition to response only where the CDI signal and field evidence converge.

How to use this tab

Use the seasonal and monthly forecast links to compare the official national outlook with the CDI for each province, district and LLG.

The satellite link can support rapid visual review of current cloud patterns and synoptic conditions, while Public Weather provides day-to-day national reference information.

Suggested interpretation

Use PNGNWS products as the official national forecast context, and use the CDI as the screening and prioritisation layer for provincial, district and LLG follow-up.

Where both the PNGNWS outlook and the dashboard indicators point in the same direction, confidence in follow-up targeting is stronger. Where they differ, additional technical review and field verification may be needed.

On-demand briefing

Generate a drought and fire report

What this does: compiles a detailed drought and fire briefing from ADAPt’s live data: the Combined Drought Index (CDI) by province, district and LLG, people living in each CDI phase, ENSO/IOD climate drivers and the CDI trend, plus this season’s fire alerts from PNG Fire Watch side by side with drought, into charts, tables and direct links to the underlying data and map layers. It runs entirely in your browser from the same public data files the rest of this dashboard uses, reflects whatever is published at the moment you click Generate, and is not saved or uploaded anywhere.
